PIECE.↗01 / THE FOUNDATIONSWhat are you actually buying when you buy a share?Read the note +

A share represents a small ownership stake in a company. Its price can change as people’s expectations about the business change.

Price alone doesn’t describe the company, its costs, or its risks. Understanding the business is part of understanding the share.

OUT.02 / THE CHARTSSame chart. Different timeframe. Different story.Read the note +

A chart groups price information into intervals. A five-minute candle describes a different slice of activity from a daily candle.

A move that looks dramatic up close may be a small part of a longer pattern. Neither view tells you what happens next.

The bid, ask, liquidity, and price movement can affect execution. The type of order is part of the decision.
