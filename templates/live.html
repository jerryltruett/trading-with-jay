Sample learning notes / Educational examples

A chart is the start of a question.

01 / Context

Zoom out first.

Compare a move with the wider trend. A short-term price change can mean something different on a longer chart.

02 / Levels

Mark the places that matter.

Look for areas where price has paused or reversed. Treat a level as an observation to examine, rather than a prediction.

03 / Risk

Know the downside.

Write down what would invalidate an idea and what you could lose before considering a trade.

These are sample educational notes, not a session transcript or a recommendation to buy or sell.
